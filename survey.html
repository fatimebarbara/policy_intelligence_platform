Future of Cities · Dr. Fatime Barbara Hegyi

Your City
Intelligence Map

Thirteen questions about the city you work in and how your institution actually behaves. At the end you get a personalised version of the Map of City Intelligence: where your system breaks the chain between evidence and action, which pillar to move first, the traps your design is exposed to, and what the work asks of you.

About eight minutes. There are no right answers — score what you observe, not what the city reports about itself. Nothing you type leaves your browser unless you choose to share the result.
